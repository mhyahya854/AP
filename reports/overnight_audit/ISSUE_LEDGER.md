### OA-001 (P1) Single/two-page modes present the 1,899 capture fragments as pages

- observed: The counter reads 'Page 23 / 1899' and 'Pages 24-25 / 1899'. Those 1,899 units are source captures, not printed pages, and one capture can be a fragment of a page.
- evidence: reports/supplied_entries_qa.json -> controls.probe.counter; source_png/SOURCE_PNG_MANIFEST.json -> record_kind says 'source_capture_partial_or_full_page_not_a_printed_page'
- root cause: The reader reused the region index as a page model because no printed-page map exists.
- confidence: certain
- correction: Relabel honestly, add a Section mode that pages through the 383 verified heading anchors, withdraw the printed-spread claim and explain why in the UI.
- patch: PATCH-1

### OA-002 (P1) Two drawers overlap on a narrow viewport with stored desktop preferences

- observed: At 390x844 with prefs {left:open,right:open} both fixed drawers are shown; each is up to 280 px wide, so they overlap and one control is obscured.
- evidence: docs/layout/13-mobile-drawers-closed.png vs the earlier 02-mobile shot; KNOWN_ISSUES item 12b
- root cause: Closed defaults are applied only when no preference has been stored.
- confidence: certain
- correction: Transiently close the other drawer when one is opened on a narrow viewport, and force both closed on a narrow load without overwriting the stored desktop preference.
- patch: PATCH-2

### OA-003 (P2) Figure credit rendered above its photograph instead of after it

- observed: 11 regions put the credit paragraph before the image, so the credit prints above the photograph; the rest of the book (211 regions) prints it after the image.
- evidence: FIGURE_TABLE_AUDIT.json -> credits_before_the_image; element_order.py on TMP-0570 shows 'Mike Kemp/Getty Images' before the <img>; the source crop shows a vertical credit at the figure's right edge
- root cause: The donor text carried the vertical credit as its own paragraph before the placeholder image.
- confidence: high
- correction: Move the credit paragraph to directly after the image, byte-identical.
- patch: PATCH-3

### OA-004 (P2) 36 regions render no content

- confidence: certain
- correction: Keep the regions (identity preserved) but stop presenting them as pages - covered by PATCH-1.
- patch: PATCH-1

- confidence: certain
- correction: Exclude docs/layout/ and other small derived QA images from LFS and commit them as ordinary blobs; leave the 1,899 source captures and the book in LFS.
